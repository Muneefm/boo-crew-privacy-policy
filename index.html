Boo Crew Privacy Policy

Halloween Ghost Cat Watch Face (Wear OS watch face) · Effective 5 October 2026

Summary

Boo Crew does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network access.

What the watch face uses

Boo Crew is built with Google's Watch Face Format, a resource-only format with no app code. The watch displays these values on screen, and they never leave your device:

  • Time, date and battery level, used to draw the clock, the date and the battery readout.
  • Heart rate and step count, provided by the watch system (Wear OS Health Services), shown under the time. Wear OS asks for your permission before the watch face can show them.
  • Weather, provided by the watch system, shown as an icon and temperature.
  • The complication you choose (for example next event or sunrise). Its data comes from the app that provides it and is covered by that app's privacy policy.

Your settings (mood and complication) are stored on the watch by Wear OS.

Permissions

The Boo Crew app requests no Android permissions itself. Health data permission is handled by Wear OS for the watch face system.

Purchases

Google Play handles purchases. We do not receive your payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google Play processes purchases.

Children

Boo Crew does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
